Highland Hideaway is a cottage on 23 acres near Haliburton, Ontario.
Three bedrooms with a queen bed in each, so it sleeps six, and one bathroom
with a tub and shower. The kitchen has a full-size range, a fridge and an
island. There is baseboard heat throughout, a wood stove in the living room,
and a screened-in deck, The Sanctuary, with a table for six.

The setting on 23 wooded acres is what makes this place special. The cottage
sits back in its own woods, private and without traffic noise, and the public
boat launch on Drag Lake is directly across the road. There is a wooden dock to swim from, and a canoe, a
paddleboard and a cart for getting them down there. You get the woods and the
water without having to pick one.

## Ten minutes to the village

Haliburton is less than a ten minute drive. Two full grocery stores, an LCBO,
hardware, two pharmacies, and a handful of restaurants, galleries and thrift
shops. Minden is a little further, and worth the drive for the river running
through the middle of it. The west gate of Algonquin Park is within a day trip
if you leave early. Downtown Toronto is about three hours away.

A week here does not have to be planned around supplies, because everything you
need is nearby. More detail is in [what is nearby](/posts/whats-nearby/).

## Autumn runs long here

The colour arrives over about three weeks and moves across the county as it
goes, so timing a visit is less precise than people expect. The
[fall colours guide](/posts/fall-colours/) covers when and where to look. Over
two weekends in October more than twenty-five artists open their studios for
the [Studio Tour](/posts/haliburton-studio-tour/).

The weeks after the leaves are down, through to the end of December, are the
quietest of the year up here, and the wood stove is the reason to come for
them. See [lighting the wood stove](/posts/lighting-the-wood-stove/) and
[a cozy night in](/posts/cozy-night-in/).

## Who you are booking from

We have had the place since 2023. We were looking for somewhere quiet that was
not too far from Toronto, and what surprised us was how much Haliburton itself
had to offer once we got there. We get up as often as we can, and when we are
not using it we would rather it was being used than sitting empty. Some of the
guests who came under the previous owners have carried on staying with us,
which we take as a reasonable sign.

## Before you book

No smoking. Quiet hours are 10pm to 7am. Bedding and towels are not provided,
so bring your own; [what to bring](/posts/what-to-bring/) has the full list.
The driveway is steep, and [getting here and parking](/posts/getting-here-and-parking/)
is worth reading before you set off rather than on arrival. Cell service is
limited. The internet is good enough to stream on one device, less so on
several.

We rent through Airbnb. This site is where the guides live.
