The public boat launch on Drag Lake is directly across the road from the
cottage. You can carry a boat down, or use the provided canoe cart, and
be on the water in a few minutes.

## The dock

The wooden dock at the launch is where most guests end up on a hot
afternoon. Sit in the sun, then get in the water.

{{< image src="/images/dock-autumn.jpg" alt="A wooden dock reaching into calm water with autumn colour on the far shore" caption="The dock at the launch" >}}

## Boats

Bring the canoe or the stand-up paddleboard down from the cottage. There's also
a canoe cart that we keep in the utility room.

In the utility room, you'll also find paddles for the canoe,
paddles for the stand-up paddleboard,
adult-sized life jackets, and orange boating safety containers.

## Conditions

We're in the narrow part of the lake, where it's rarely very windy. To the east, lake opens
up very wide and the wind can pick up there. We usually plan to paddle up-wind first, so that the trip
back is easier.

The launch is public, but there is minimal boat traffic through the day.

## Safety

Ontario law requires a personal flotation device for every person aboard,
plus a bailer, a sound signalling device, and a buoyant heaving line. Bring the orange
boating safety container.

Tell someone at the cottage where you are going and when you expect to be
back.

## Best seasons

Early May, after ice-out and before the black flies arrive, and mid-August after the bugs have subsided.
