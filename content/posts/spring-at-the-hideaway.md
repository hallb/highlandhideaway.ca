Spring is the shortest season here and the one most guests miss.

## Trilliums

White trilliums come up across the property in May, thickest along the
tracks into the forest. They are the provincial flower of Ontario and it is
poor form to pick them: a trillium that loses its leaves can take years to
recover.

{{< image src="/images/trillium-white-cluster.jpg" alt="A cluster of white trilliums among leaf litter" caption="White trilliums, mid-May" >}}

Red trilliums grow here too, in smaller numbers and usually in shadier
ground.

{{< image src="/images/trillium-red.jpg" alt="A single red trillium in flower" caption="Red trillium" >}}

## What to expect

The driveway is fine through the thaw, but the parking area can be soft.

Black flies arrive in mid-May and last a few weeks. They are worst at dusk
and near water. Bring repellent if you are coming then, and see
[what to bring](/posts/what-to-bring/).

The screened-in deck, The Sanctuary, is the one place outside they cannot
reach, which is why it's a popular space in the spring.

{{< image src="/images/sanctuary-wide.jpg" alt="The screened-in deck with table, chairs, and forest beyond the screens" caption="The Sanctuary" >}}

The lake opens up well before the black flies arrive, which makes early May some of
the best paddling of the year. The launch is across the road; see the
[canoe launch guide](/posts/canoe-launch-guide/).

{{< image src="/images/lake-spring.jpg" alt="Open lake water and forested hills under a bright sky" caption="Early May on the water" >}}
