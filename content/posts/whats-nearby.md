The cottage is near Haliburton, Ontario, in the Haliburton Highlands.

The closest thing to it is the water. The public boat launch on Drag Lake
is directly across the road, with a dock to swim from. See the
[canoe launch guide](/posts/canoe-launch-guide/).


## Haliburton

Less than a ten minute drive. Two full grocery stores, an LCBO, hardware, two
pharmacies, and a handful of restaurants, galleries, and thrift shops.

Foodland is on Highland Street, the main strip. Todd's Your Independent
Grocer is on the far side of town, and McFaddens, a good butcher, is next
door to it.

## Minden

A little further, and worth the drive for the river through the middle of
town. Pride in the Highlands runs each summer across Minden and Haliburton;
see [Pride in the Highlands](/posts/minden-pride/).

## Algonquin Park

The west gate is within a reasonable day trip. Going for the day means
leaving early.

## Groceries and supplies

Everything you need is in Haliburton, ten minutes away. See the
[pantry guide](/posts/cottage-pantry-guide/) for what is already at the
cottage.

## Getting outside

The Haliburton Highlands has a large network of rail trails and forest
roads. Try the Haliburton County Rail Trail which starts just off
Gelert Road near Highway 21 or the Head Lake Loop, which is 6 km
through the college and the far side of Head Lake and back. The
Barnum Creek Nature Reserve is privately run, has many well-maintained trails
and takes donations at the entrance.

For quieter suggestions, see
[hidden gems nearby](/posts/hidden-gems-nearby/).
