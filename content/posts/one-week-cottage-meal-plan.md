## The plan

| Day | Dinner |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Pasta with a jar sauce, because you arrived late |
| 2 | BBQ chicken thighs, potatoes, and whatever vegetable looked good |
| 3 | Chicken sandwiches and soup from day two |
| 4 | Chili, made in the morning, eaten by the fire |
| 5 | Burgers or sausages |
| 6 | Fish, with rice |
| 7 | Whatever is left, fried together |

## Breakfasts

Eggs, bread, oats, fruit. Ground coffee is provided, so you only need to
bring it if you are particular.

## Lunches

Sandwiches, leftovers, and cheese. If you are out for the day, making lunch
before you leave saves a detour back into town.

## Notes on the kitchen

The oven is a full-size range and the fridge holds a week of food for six.
There is one sink and no dishwasher, so think one-pot meals.

See [cooking at the cottage](/posts/cooking-at-the-cottage/) for the layout
and [the pantry guide](/posts/cottage-pantry-guide/) for what is already
here.
