You have the whole cottage and the whole property. A couple of closets are locked, as is the red shed.

## Getting in

There is a lock box to the right of the front door. We'll send you the code
after you book, and we recommend leaving the key in the box when you're not
using it.

## Check-in and check-out

Check-in is 3 PM. Check-out is 11 AM.

## Utilities

- **Heat.** Baseboard heaters in each room, plus the wood stove. See
  [lighting the wood stove](/posts/lighting-the-wood-stove/).
- **Water.** Water comes from the well and is potable. In addition the water is filtered
  as it enters the house.
- **Septic.** The cottage is on a septic system. Nothing but toilet paper
  goes down the toilet.
- **Garbage and recycling.** Please take these with you when you leave. You can drop them off
  at one of the landfills. We provide a one-time pass for you. Please see [the municipality's landfill page for locations and times](https://www.dysartetal.ca/living-in-our-community/garbage-and-recycling/landfills/)

## If you need us

Message us through Airbnb, or our contact information is in the House Manual which is a white book on the
bookshelf.
