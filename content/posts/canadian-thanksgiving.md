## Someone else's house

Thanksgiving at home means hosting. It means the shopping, the cooking, the
table, and the dishwasher run at eleven at night after everyone has gone.

Thanksgiving in a rented cottage means most of that stops being your
problem. You still cook. You don't clean up after twelve people in your own
kitchen, and nobody has to drive home afterwards.

The kitchen here is open to the dining and living space, so whoever is
cooking is still in the room with everyone else. There are three bedrooms
with queen beds, plus a cot and a child trundle bed. See
[sleeping arrangements](/posts/sleeping-arrangements/) for what that means in
practice, and [cooking at the cottage](/posts/cooking-at-the-cottage/) for
what the kitchen has in it.

Bring the food with you. The village is ten minutes away, but the second
weekend of October is not the weekend to discover you're missing something.
The [cottage pantry guide](/posts/cottage-pantry-guide/) covers what's here
already.

## The weekend itself

Thanksgiving Monday is 12 October in 2026, so the long weekend runs
Saturday 10 to Monday 12.

It lands in harvest season, and usually at or near peak colour, though the
turn moves by a week or two from year to year. The lakes are still open and
the water is quiet, because most of the summer traffic has gone. It's colder
than you expect in the evenings, which is what the wood stove is for.

The [Studio Tour](/posts/haliburton-studio-tour/) runs that Saturday and
Sunday. If you want one thing to do that isn't eating, that's the one.

Spend Thanksgiving somewhere worth being thankful for.
