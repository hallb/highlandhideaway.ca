There are three bedrooms, all on the same floor, each with a queen sized bed. Each room sleeps two comfortably.


## The side room

A pine bed frame under the window, with a dresser and a mirror.

## The corner room

A darker bed frame, a chest of drawers.

{{< image src="/images/bedroom-b-window.jpg" alt="Bedroom with a dark wood bed frame beside a window" caption="The corner room" >}}

## The main bedroom

The largest of the three, with a large dresser and framed park posters.

{{< image src="/images/bedroom-c-window.jpg" alt="Bedroom with a dark bed, closet doors, and a green dresser" caption="The main bedroom" >}}

## Linens

You'll need to bring your own towels and queen-sized linens (fitted and flat sheets, pillow cases, and duvet covers). The closets in the side and corner rooms have blankets/duvets to put into your duvet covers; and each bed has two pillows.

## The bathroom

One bathroom, shared, with a tub and shower.

{{< image src="/images/bathroom-tub.jpg" alt="Bathroom with a tub and shower behind a curtain" caption="The bathroom" >}}

The hot water tank is a normal residential size.
