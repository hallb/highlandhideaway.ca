The cottage sleeps six. There are three bedrooms, all on the same floor, each
with a queen bed.

There is also a folding bed in the side room's closet, and a trundle under the
bed in the main bedroom. Both are singles. They are there so a group can divide
itself up differently, not to take the number past six.

## Which room to take

It matters less than you would think. All three are close in size, and the main
bedroom is only a little larger than the other two. Each room has a ceiling fan
and curtains that darken it properly during the day.

## Heat and cooling

Baseboard heaters and thermostats keep every room comfortable, with or without
the wood stove running.

Summer takes a bit of management. Open the windows at night to let the place
cool down, then watch the temperature in the morning: as it climbs outside,
close them again unless there is a breeze. Opening the windows does not cool
the cottage when it is hotter outside than in. There are ceiling fans in the
bedrooms and portable fans you can move to wherever you need one.

## Linens

We don't supply linens. Bring queen-sized sheets, pillow cases, duvet covers,
and your own towels. Pillows and duvets are here — the closets in the side and
corner rooms hold the duvets, and there are two pillows to a bed. If you plan
to use the folding bed or the trundle, bring single bedding for those too.

## Children

There is no crib and no pack-n-play at the cottage, so bring one if you need it.

## The bathroom

One bathroom, shared, with a tub and shower. There is no second toilet, so six
people work around one room in the morning. It is worth agreeing an order the
night before.
