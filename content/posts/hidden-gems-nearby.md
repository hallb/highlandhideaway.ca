Here are some of our favourite places.


## A quiet lookout

The [Skyline Road Lookout](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/canada/ontario/skyline-road-lookout) has views over
the town and Head Lake.

## Somewhere to swim that is not busy

In addition to swimming at the dock across the road, we recommend two other locations:

* [Furnace Falls](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kXFCQPQTgWpAdScF8) is about a 30 minute drive away.
* [Sand Point Public Beach](https://maps.app.goo.gl/J3MWVorUwPpqngyq6) is a fair paddle across Drag Lake, or accessible by car if you head back through Haliburton.


## A place to eat that is not in town

Two very different places that are right next to each other

* [Kate's Burger Counter](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HTMNGvFUvuumsV1o9) $$
* [Post House](https://haliburtonposthouse.ca/dining/) $$$$

## On the property

Some of it does not require a drive. In May the trilliums come up across
the property, thickest along the tracks into the forest. See
[spring at the hideaway](/posts/spring-at-the-hideaway/).

There's a trail that goes up the hill starting behind the red shed. It
has blue blazes that you can easily follow. The first hundred meters up the
hill are very steep, but it's easier after that. Plus, we have walking sticks
available by the front door.
