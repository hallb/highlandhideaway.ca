The kitchen is set up for cooking real meals, not just reheating them.

## What is here

- A full-size electric range and oven
- A full-size fridge and freezer
- A microwave, kettle, a drip coffee maker (with Kicking Horse ground coffee provided),
 toaster and toaster oven
- An island with counter space and seating around the kitchen table
- A propane BBQ on the deck outside the kitchen (propane provided)

There is no dishwasher. Washing up is by hand, and the drying rack lives
under the sink.

{{< image src="/images/kitchen-dining.jpg" alt="The kitchen seen from the dining area, with the island in the foreground" caption="The kitchen from the dining side" >}}

## Layout

Pots and pans are in the lower cabinets. Cups and glassware are above the
counter to the right of the sink. Cutlery is in the drawer to the left of the sink.
Dishes are to the left above the sink.

## Eating

The dining table inside seats six. And the screened-in deck ("The Sanctuary") has a table that
can seat six as well. That's where we usually have coffee in the morning all year round, except
for the coldest winter mornings.

{{< image src="/images/sanctuary-table.jpg" alt="The table on the screened deck, six chairs, string lights overhead" caption="The Sanctuary, set for six" >}}

## Water and the septic system

The cottage is on a septic system. No grease down the drain, and no food
scraps.
