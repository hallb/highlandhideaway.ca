[Pride in the Highlands](https://www.prideinthehighlands.ca/) (formerly Minden Pride) runs each summer in Minden and Haliburton, a short drive from the cottage.

{{< image src="/images/pride-float.jpg" alt="People in rainbow colours floating down the river beneath a Minden Pride banner" caption="2021 on the Gull River in Minden, passing under the bridge" >}}


## What happens

The week includes events around Minden and Haliburton, and a float down the
Gull River that passes under the bridges in the middle of Minden.

## Going

The river float is the busiest part of the week.

## If you are staying with us

The dates are usually in August. If you are booking around them, check the
[Pride site](https://www.prideinthehighlands.ca/) first and then check availability.
