## Why there is so much of this here

The Highlands has a working arts community rather than a gift-shop version
of one. People live here and make things here, and a good number of them
will talk to you about the work if you turn up.

The Haliburton School of Fine Arts started in 1967. Its first courses ran at
Royal View Lodge on Lake Kashagawigamog, before it had a building of its
own. It became an extension division of Sir Sandford Fleming College shortly
after, though sources disagree on whether that was late 1967 or 1969. The
campus moved to Glebe Park, above Head Lake, in 2004. It's now the
Haliburton School of Art + Design.

The order matters. The school grew out of a local arts community that was
already there; it didn't invent one.

I've taken a course there myself.

Glebe Park also holds the Haliburton Sculpture Forest, with more than
twenty-five permanent works along the trails. It's free, it's open
year-round, and it fills an hour without any planning.

## How the tour works

The tour is self-guided. You drive it yourself, in whatever order suits you,
and the studios are open 10am to 5pm on both weekends:

- Saturday 3 and Sunday 4 October 2026
- Saturday 10 and Sunday 11 October 2026

It runs roughly east to west, from Tory Hill to Carnarvon, across about
twenty-one studios. The work covers pottery and ceramics, glass, fine
jewellery, textile and fabric art, painting, leatherwork, photography,
quilting, and metal art.

Some of these studios are only open to visitors on these two weekends of the
year. That's the part worth planning around.

Several of them are down back roads, tucked into the forest or sitting on a
lake, so give yourself more driving time than the map suggests.

The Haliburton Highlands Artist Exhibition runs at The Space in Haliburton
from 18 September to 18 October 2026, which covers both tour weekends.

## Booking it

The second tour weekend is also the Canadian Thanksgiving long weekend,
Saturday 10 to Monday 12 October. You can do the tour on the Saturday or
Sunday and still have the Monday. See
[Thanksgiving at the Hideaway](/posts/canadian-thanksgiving/).

We're a ten minute drive from the village of Haliburton, which puts most of
the tour within easy reach for a day.
