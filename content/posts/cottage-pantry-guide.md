The pantry carries the things that are annoying to buy in small amounts.
Plan to bring everything else.

## On the shelf

- Salt, pepper, and a few dried herbs
- Cooking oil
- Sugar
- Tea, coffee, and coffee filters

## In the drawers

Foil, plastic wrap, parchment, tea towels, and cloths.

## By the sink

Dish soap, hand soap, and a sponge. Under the sink is a drying rack for dishes.

## What to buy on the way

There are two full grocery stores in Haliburton, which is
less than a 10 minute drive away. So while you don't want to drive back
and forth needlessly, it isn't too bad if you forget the milk.

The Foodland is on Highland Street (the main strip in Haliburton), and the Loblaws
("Todd's Your Independent Grocer") is on the the other side of town. Next to the
Loblaws is a very nice butcher, McFaddens.

See the [one-week meal plan](/posts/one-week-cottage-meal-plan/) for a shopping
list that works.

## Leaving

Please take perishables with you. Rubbish and recycling go with you too;
see [guest access](/posts/guest-access/) for the landfill pass.
