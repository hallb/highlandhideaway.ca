The wood stove supplements the baseboard heat in the living room and the cottage. It is not
difficult, but it rewards doing the steps in order.

## Before you light it

Check that the flue is open. The handle is on the front top, and needs to be pulled
out.

Firewood for the wood stove is stacked on the deck by the sliding door, or under the roof of
the red shed (the wood stacked beside the fire pit is a lower quality and only for campfires).
Kindling is under the roof of the red shed too. There are fire starters in
the grey box next to the TV.

## Lighting it

1. Open the flue fully.
2. Lay two or three splits at the back, with a gap between them.
3. Put a fire starter or crumpled paper in front, then kindling over it in a
   loose pile. Loose matters more than neat.
4. Light the paper and leave the door open a finger's width until the
   kindling is properly going.
5. Close the door. Leave the air intake open until the splits have caught.
6. Once it is burning steadily, damp the air intake back.

If it smokes into the room, the flue is closed or the chimney is cold. Open
the flue and burn a twist of paper high in the firebox to start the draw.

## Keeping it going

Add wood before the previous load is fully down to coals. A stove that has
gone to ash takes as long to restart as a cold one.

## Shutting down

Let it burn down. Do not close the flue on a live fire.

## Safety

- Nothing dries on the stove. Nothing.
- Ash goes in the metal pail, and the pail goes outside.
- There are smoke and carbon monoxide alarms in the cottage.
