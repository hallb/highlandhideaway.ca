November is quiet here, and that is most of its appeal. Even so, every
Saturday this month has something on: a film festival, a garden lit for the
season, and a Christmas market.

## Friday 6 to Sunday 8 November: Haliburton International Film Festival

Three days and seven films at the Northern Lights Performing Arts Pavilion
in Haliburton, ten minutes from the cottage. The lineup had not been
announced at the last check.

A VIP pass is $65 for every film, a day pass is $32, and single tickets are
$10 a film. There is more in
[where to watch a movie near Haliburton](/posts/movies-near-haliburton/), and
everything official is at
[thoseothermovies.com/hiff](https://www.thoseothermovies.com/hiff).

Those Other Movies also runs a film night on the second Thursday of most
months, but not in November, because the festival takes the month. The
next one is Thursday 10 December.

## Saturday 14 November, and three more nights: Abbey Gardens Holiday Nights

Abbey Gardens, at 1012 Garden Gate Dr in Haliburton, is 20 minutes from the
cottage. It puts Christmas lights through the gardens on four Saturday
evenings, 4 to 8pm each time. Their poster says admission is free. It is
outdoors in November, so dress for it.

- **Saturday 14 November** is the opening night, a holiday night market with
  local artisan vendors, munchies and crafts, and Santa photos.
- **Saturday 21 November, Saturday 28 November and Saturday 5 December** are
  the three more nights. They have the garden lights and shopping at The Food
  Hub, again with munchies, crafts and Santa photos.

Their event page is at [abbeygardens.ca](https://abbeygardens.ca/).

## Saturday 21 November: Haliburton Christmas Market

10am to 3pm, in two places: the A.J. LaRue Arena at 728 Mountain St, and the
Haliburton Legion across the street. The vendors sell handmade goods, gifts
and local businesses' products, so it suits an early start on Christmas
shopping.

It is a five minute drive from the cottage. Park in the arena's lot, or on
the street nearby, and walk across the street to the Legion.

The event description says the arena is reachable by
stairs only, and that the Legion is fully accessible. If you need step-free
access, plan around the Legion.

Abbey Gardens opens at 4pm the same day, an hour after the market closes.

## The rest of the month

The clocks go back on Sunday 1 November, so it gets dark earlier. That makes
the wood stove the main event on most evenings. See
[lighting the wood stove](/posts/lighting-the-wood-stove/) and
[a cozy night in](/posts/cozy-night-in/).

## Staying for it

The arena and the Legion are five minutes from us, the pavilion is ten and
Abbey Gardens is twenty, so all three are short outings from the cottage.
The festival is the easy fit for a weekend.

On 21 November the market and the garden fit into one day, with an hour
between them.
