American Thanksgiving is Thursday 26 November in 2026. Canada does not
observe it, which turns out to be the whole point of coming here for it.

## Nothing is closed

The last Thursday in November is an ordinary working Thursday in Ontario.
Canadian Thanksgiving was back in October. The shops are open, the roads
are empty of holiday traffic, and nobody else is trying to book a table
anywhere.

If you have spent a Thanksgiving in a full airport, this is the opposite of
that.

## What late November is actually like

The leaves are down and the snow has usually not settled, so this is the
bare part of the year. The forest is grey and brown and you can see a long
way into it, further than at any other time.

It is dark early. Sunset is in the late afternoon by the end of November,
so the evening is most of the day.

## Driving up

The cottage is about three hours from downtown Toronto, and the drive from
a border crossing is longer than that. See
[getting here and parking](/posts/getting-here-and-parking/).

<!-- Q6: measure and state the drive from the crossings US guests actually

Bring a passport. A US passport card or an enhanced driver's licence also
works at a land crossing, but a regular driver's licence does not.

## Tires and the driveway

Ontario has no winter tire law. Quebec does, and US guests sometimes arrive
expecting the same rule here. What decides it is the driveway rather than
the highway: it is gravel, it is steep, and getting up it in snow needs
all-wheel drive and snow tires. If you cannot get up, park near the bottom
clear of the neighbours and walk up.
