It rains. This is the room for it.

The living room has the wood stove, sofas enough for everyone, and a
walkout to the screened-in deck in case you want to smell the fresh rain.

## Getting warm

Light the stove. It takes about twenty minutes to make a real difference to
the room and about an hour to warm the rest of the cottage. See
[lighting the wood stove](/posts/lighting-the-wood-stove/).

## What is here

There's a TV with which you can log into your own streaming service. There's also
a collection of about 200 CDs with a CD player and radio. In the drawers under the TV
we have a variety of card games and board games from Uno to Chess to Settlers of Catan.

The bookshelf by the front door has a selection of books and novels.

## Bandwidth

The cottage has internet access which works for streaming, but it is not very high bandwidth,
so multiple streaming devices at the same time can overload it.

## The Sanctuary

The screened-in deck is the best seat in the cottage in bad weather, and
the only one outdoors that the black flies cannot reach. It has a table for
six, and it is where most guests end up having coffee.
