The cottage is near Haliburton, Ontario, about a 3 hour drive from downtown Toronto.
We'll provide the specific address once you've booked.


## The last stretch

The driveway is gravel and steep, at the top is a parking area.

Any car will make it in summer.

## Parking

Park in the gravel area beside the shed. There is room for at least three vehicles
without blocking the turnaround. The septic bed is on the far side of the cottage, so you don't
have to worry about accidentally driving on it.

## In winter

In winter, the driveway is ploughed within a day after snowfall, but you will
need AWD and snow tires to get up. If you
cannot drive up, just park on the driveway near the bottom out of the way of our neighbours,
and walk up the hill.

See [winter at the hideaway](/posts/winter-at-the-hideaway/) for what else
changes in the cold months.
