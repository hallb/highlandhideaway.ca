## The Cord

The Corduroy Enduro has run since 1953. The organisers call it Canada's
National Enduro, and about 700 racers enter it, riding roughly 300
kilometres of trail through the Haliburton Highlands for a $13,500 pro
purse and a Cord Log. Riders come from across North America. In 2026 it
runs 17 to 20 September, out of Gooderham.

Gooderham is a village. The race is far larger than the place it starts
from, and what a rider actually needs for the weekend is somewhere to put a
trailer, water to wash a bike, somewhere for wet gear to dry, and a kitchen
that works at five in the morning and again at nine at night. That is an
accommodation problem rather than a village problem, and it is worth
solving before entries close rather than after.

We are 22 minutes from Gooderham.

## The 2026 event

The 2026 Corduroy runs 17 to 20 September at Gooderham, Ontario. Around 700
riders, roughly 300 kilometres of trail, and a $13,500 pro purse. The
weekend carries a Friday prologue and a final motocross for the pro class
around the main racing, and there are demo rides, a youth enduro and a
trail ride alongside it.

Entries, the schedule and everything official are at
[corduroyenduro.ca](https://www.corduroyenduro.ca). We have no connection
to the event. This page is about where to sleep.

## Watching it

Riders are well served by the event's own materials. The people travelling
with them are not, and they are often the ones deciding where the weekend
is based.

The course is spread across the countryside around Gooderham, and the racing
is watched from a handful of named points. These are the ones the organisers
publish:

- **The Friday prologue.** Short course, run before the main racing, with
  endurocross sections, motocross and hill climbs. Riders set a time on it
  and those times decide the starting order for the next morning.
- **MacDuff's Grasstrack.** One of the first tests of the weekend, run on
  grass, which makes it one of the easier ones to follow from the side.
- **Burnt River Crossing.** A river crossing over slippery rock. The
  organisers describe it as infamous, and it is the one where the day goes
  wrong for somebody.
- **Sedgewick's MX.** An open sand and gravel pit with trail mixed into it.
  Riders also stop here to refuel, so there is more to watch than the
  racing.
- **Green's Mountain.** Up, then down, then up and down again. The vantage
  points are the best of the weekend. **The walk in is hard**, and the
  organisers say so themselves. Do not take somebody who will struggle with
  it, and do not attempt it in the wrong footwear.
- **The Butt Test.** An endurocross test, ridden by every class.
- **The final MX.** Back at the start area, where the pro class races a
  last time to finish the weekend.

Spectator maps, directions to each point and the full schedule are released
closer to the event and were not published when this was written. They go
up on the
[spectator information page](https://www.corduroyenduro.ca/blank-5-1-1-2-1-1),
which is the page to check in the week before you travel.

## Getting there and stocking up

Gooderham is 22 minutes from us. Haliburton is closer, under ten minutes,
and it is where almost everything you will want is. See
[what is nearby](/posts/whats-nearby/).

There is an LCBO in Gooderham and another in Haliburton. The Haliburton one
is better stocked, so if the order matters, do that stop on the way in
rather than on the way to the course.

## Making a weekend of it

Not everybody in the vehicle is there for the motorcycles.

Late September is a good week to be in the Highlands on its own terms. The
lakes have gone quiet, the summer traffic has gone home, and the colour is
usually starting without having peaked. The Cord weekend sits about a
fortnight ahead of the Thanksgiving and Studio Tour traffic that fills the
county in October.

How far along the colour is that particular weekend is not something anyone
can promise in advance. See
[fall colours in the Haliburton Highlands](/posts/fall-colours/) for what
turns when and which reports to check in the week before you travel.
