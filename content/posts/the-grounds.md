The cottage sits in a clearing on the property. Most of the 23 acres is
mixed forest.

The public boat launch on Drag Lake is directly across the road. See the
[canoe launch guide](/posts/canoe-launch-guide/).

## The fire pit

The fire pit is ringed with stone and surrounded by wooden benches. The
wood stacked beside it is for campfires. The better wood, for the stove
indoors, is on the deck by the sliding door and under the roof of the red
shed.

{{< image src="/images/fire-pit-yard.jpg" alt="The fire pit area with stacked firewood and benches" caption="Campfire wood, kept beside the pit" >}}

See [how to start a campfire](/posts/how-to-start-a-campfire/) if the wood
is damp, which it often is in spring.

## The swing

There is a round net swing hung from a maple at the edge of the clearing.
Nothing is posted on it and we do not know what it will hold, so use some
judgement about who gets on it and how many at once.

## Trails

A trail starts behind the red shed and climbs the hill, marked with blue
blazes that are easy to follow. The first hundred metres are steep and it
eases after that. Walking sticks are by the front door.

In winter the same trail is good for snowshoeing. See
[winter at the hideaway](/posts/winter-at-the-hideaway/).

## The shed

The red shed beside the driveway is locked, but the firewood and kindling
stacked under its roof are there for you to use.
